# PRISMA Flow Description

## Identification

Research papers were identified using IEEE Xplore, SpringerLink, Scopus, and Web of Science databases.

The search strings combined cybersecurity protocols, OCC systems, autonomous systems, Industry 4.0, and performance evaluation keywords.

---

## Screening

After removing duplicate studies, title and abstract screening was performed.

The screening focused on:

- secure communication
- OCC systems
- industrial cybersecurity
- protocol evaluation
- latency and resilience analysis

---

## Eligibility

Eligible full-text papers were reviewed in detail.

The studies were evaluated for:

- technical relevance
- cybersecurity methodology
- protocol implementation
- KPI measurements
- attack analysis

---

## Included Studies

Final papers were selected for qualitative analysis, comparative study, and framework development.

The selected studies form the foundation for the proposed OCC cybersecurity middleware framework.
