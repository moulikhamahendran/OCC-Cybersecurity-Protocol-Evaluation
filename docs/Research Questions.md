# Research Questions

## RQ1

What security threats, security mechanisms, and real-time communication requirements characterize OCC architectures for autonomous and industrial systems?

## RQ2

How can cybersecurity protocols be systematically evaluated for robustness and real-time performance in realistic OCC environments?

## RQ3

How do different secure communication protocols behave under normal and attack conditions regarding latency, jitter, throughput, packet loss, and resilience?

## Motivation

Modern OCC systems require secure and reliable communication between distributed autonomous devices and central monitoring infrastructure.

The increasing use of Industry 4.0 technologies introduces cybersecurity challenges that directly affect system reliability and operational safety.
