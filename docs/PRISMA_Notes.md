# PRISMA Analysis

## PRISMA 2020 Flow Summary

This systematic literature review followed the PRISMA methodology for identifying, screening, and selecting relevant research papers related to cybersecurity protocols and OCC architectures.

---

# Screening Phase

## Title and Abstract Screening

Records were screened based on:

- relevance to cybersecurity
- OCC architectures
- Industry 4.0 systems
- autonomous systems
- protocol performance evaluation

## Records Excluded During Screening

Common exclusion reasons:

- unrelated application domains
- non-technical studies
- papers without performance evaluation
- papers lacking cybersecurity focus
- duplicate conference/journal versions

Estimated records excluded during title and abstract screening:

- 495 records excluded

## Records Moved to Full-Text Review

44 papers selected for full-text eligibility assessment.

---

# Eligibility Phase

## Full-Text Assessment Criteria

The following criteria were evaluated:

- secure communication focus
- real-time communication relevance
- KPI evaluation
- protocol comparison
- attack resilience analysis
- Industry 4.0 or OCC applicability

## Full-Text Exclusions

Reasons for exclusion:

- insufficient technical details
- no measurable KPIs
- unrelated protocol architecture
- unavailable full text
- duplicate methodology papers

Estimated exclusions after full-text review:

- 12 papers excluded

32 studies included in qualitative and comparative analysis.
