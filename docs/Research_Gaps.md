# Research Gaps

## Identified Gaps

- Limited studies combining cybersecurity and real-time KPIs
- Lack of OCC-focused cybersecurity frameworks
- Few practical implementations using lightweight hardware
- Limited attack simulation studies for OCC architectures
- Insufficient comparison between DDS, MQTT, and OPC UA in OCC systems

## Future Research Direction

Develop a measurable cybersecurity middleware framework capable of evaluating protocol resilience and real-time performance in practical OCC environments.
