# Risks and Challenges

This document identifies potential risks, limitations, and challenges associated with the research project.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to track factors that may affect:

- Research progress
- Experimental validation
- Protocol comparison accuracy
- Dataset availability
- Thesis completion timeline

---

## Research Risks

| Risk | Description | Impact |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Limited DDS datasets | Fewer public DDS benchmark datasets available | Medium |
| Protocol implementation complexity | DDS and OPC UA setup complexity | High |
| Large literature volume | Excessive number of papers during screening | Medium |
| Time constraints | Balancing research and coursework | High |
| Experimental reproducibility | Difficulty reproducing protocol conditions | Medium |

---

## Technical Challenges

### MQTT Challenges
- Broker dependency
- Security overhead with TLS
- Limited deterministic communication

### OPC UA Challenges
- Higher protocol complexity
- Increased latency in secure mode
- Complex industrial configuration

### DDS Challenges
- Difficult configuration
- High resource consumption
- Limited beginner documentation

---

## Dataset Challenges

| Challenge | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Dataset imbalance | Attack traffic may dominate or be limited |
| Missing protocol-specific datasets | Limited DDS-specific cybersecurity datasets |
| Industrial data access | Restricted real industrial network traffic |
| Data labeling issues | Inconsistent annotations |

---

## Experimental Challenges

- Simulating realistic industrial environments
- Configuring identical protocol conditions
- Measuring fair KPI comparisons
- Modeling cyberattack scenarios
- Resource limitations during benchmarking

---

## Validation Challenges

| Challenge | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Reproducibility | Results may vary across environments |
| Benchmark fairness | Ensuring identical protocol conditions |
| Security evaluation complexity | Difficult to quantify attack resistance |

---

## Research Limitations

- Reliance on literature-based comparison
- Limited access to real industrial infrastructure
- Potential differences across protocol implementations
- Variability in published benchmark results

---

## Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Limited datasets | Use multiple benchmark sources |
| Protocol complexity | Use documented open-source implementations |
| Time constraints | Follow structured roadmap |
| Literature overload | Use PRISMA filtering |
| Validation uncertainty | Compare with published studies |

---

## Expected Outcome

Risk tracking supports:

- Better planning
- Improved research transparency
- Realistic expectations
- Stronger thesis discussion section
- Better experimental preparation
