# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria

Studies were included if they satisfied the following conditions:

- Peer-reviewed journal articles and conference papers
- Publications between 2015–2026
- Studies evaluating DDS Security, MQTT with TLS, or OPC UA Security
- Research addressing cybersecurity in Operational Control Centres (OCC), autonomous vehicles, or industrial control systems
- Studies analyzing cyber attacks such as:
  - Denial of Service (DoS)
  - Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)
  - Data injection attacks
- Research measuring:
  - Latency
  - Jitter
  - Throughput
  - Resilience
  - Efficiency
- Empirical studies including:
  - Experimental research
  - Simulation-based analysis
  - Testbed evaluation
- English language publications

---

## Exclusion Criteria

Studies were excluded if they met one or more of the following conditions:

- Non-peer-reviewed publications (excluding grey literature from reputable sources)
- Publications before 2015
- Studies not addressing DDS, MQTT, or OPC UA protocols
- Research unrelated to real-time or safety-critical communication systems
- Studies focusing only on theoretical models without empirical validation
- Duplicate publications across databases
- Publications not available in English or German
- Studies without clear methodology or measurable results
- Papers lacking cybersecurity relevance
- Research without protocol comparison or evaluation focus

---

## Screening Process

The literature review followed these stages:

1. Database search using predefined search strings
2. Title screening
3. Abstract screening
4. Full-text assessment
5. Duplicate removal
6. Application of inclusion and exclusion criteria
7. Final paper selection

---

## Selection Workflow

The systematic literature review process follows:

```text
Database Search
        ↓
Title Screening
        ↓
Abstract Screening
        ↓
Full-Text Review
        ↓
Duplicate Removal
        ↓
Inclusion/Exclusion Filtering
        ↓
Final Literature Set
```

---

## Purpose

The inclusion and exclusion criteria ensure:

- High-quality scientific literature selection
- Relevance to cybersecurity protocol evaluation
- Reliable comparison across communication protocols
- Strong foundation for framework development
- Reduced bias in literature selection
