# Research Questions

## RQ1
What are cybersecurity protocols, and what role do they play in protecting communication systems?

## RQ2
What methodologies are used to evaluate these protocols in research and practical scenarios?

## RQ3
How can these protocols be validated against common cyber attacks in communication systems?

---

## Research Objective

The objective of this research is to study cybersecurity communication protocols and evaluate their effectiveness in secure communication environments.

The study focuses on:

- Understanding protocol security mechanisms
- Identifying evaluation methodologies
- Comparing protocol performance
- Validating protocol resistance against cyber threats
- Supporting secure communication framework development
