## Core MQTT Security Papers

### 1. MQTT Vulnerabilities, Attack Vectors and Solutions in the Internet of Things (IoT)
- **Year:** 2023  
- **Type:** Journal Article  
- **Focus:** MQTT vulnerabilities, DoS, spoofing, information disclosure  
- **Importance:** Foundational MQTT security paper  

### 2. Securing the IoT Application Layer From an MQTT Protocol Perspective: Challenges and Research Prospects
- **Year:** 2024  
- **Type:** Survey Paper  
- **Focus:** MQTT attacks, defense mechanisms, protocol security taxonomy  
- **Importance:** Major survey for MQTT cybersecurity  

### 3. Assessment Platform of Cybersecurity Attacks against the MQTT Protocol using SIEM
- **Year:** 2022  
- **Type:** Conference Paper  
- **Focus:** MQTT attacks with TLS performance impact  
- **Importance:** Useful for KPI evaluation and attack analysis  

### 4. Online-Semisupervised Neural Anomaly Detector to Identify MQTT-Based Attacks in Real Time
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Type:** Journal Article  
- **Focus:** Real-time anomaly detection using AI  
- **Importance:** IDS methodology for MQTT  

### 5. Security-Oriented Load Distribution in MQTTv5
- **Year:** 2025  
- **Type:** Conference Paper  
- **Focus:** MQTTv5 trust mechanism and scalability security  
- **Importance:** Advanced MQTT security architecture
